# Privacy notice outline — counsel review required

## Document metadata

- Controller legal name, identification, address, and contact.
- Privacy/data-protection contact and complaint channel.
- Version, effective date, locale, and prior-version archive.

## Plain-language summary

Explain in one screen:

- What GlucoCare does.
- Which health data it stores.
- Which data stays on the device and which may go to the cloud.
- When a trusted contact receives information.
- How to exercise data rights.

## Detailed sections

1. **Who is responsible for the data.**
2. **Data categories:** identity, health, medication, appointments, contacts, location, device/security, support, and optional analytics.
3. **Purposes and necessity:** map every category to a specific purpose.
4. **Sensitive data:** state the optional nature and consequences where Colombian law requires.
5. **Sources:** user, device, imported integration, trusted contact, clinician where applicable.
6. **Recipients/processors:** hosting, notification, email/SMS, support, analytics if approved; link current subprocessors.
7. **Trusted-contact sharing:** event types, fields, channels, and revocation.
8. **International transfer/transmission:** countries, safeguards, and contractual basis.
9. **Security:** high-level safeguards without revealing exploit details.
10. **Retention/deletion:** category-specific periods and backup expiry.
11. **Rights:** access, update, correction, proof of authorization, information about use, complaint channels, revocation, and deletion where applicable.
12. **Children/dependants:** only include after the legal/identity model is approved.
13. **Automated decisions:** state that MVP has no automated diagnosis/dosing; explain deterministic care-plan classifications.
14. **Changes:** how material changes are notified and when new consent is requested.
15. **Contact and supervisory authority.**

## Engineering evidence to attach

- Data-flow diagram and inventory.
- Consent/version records.
- Vendor and subprocessor list.
- Retention schedule.
- Export/deletion runbooks.
- Security and incident-response summaries.
